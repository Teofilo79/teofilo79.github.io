Swiched phone to monochrome mode. No colors. And the magic started happening.<br><br>
Reading news now takes less time. Like in old paper newspaper, you are not distracted ny the colorful illustrations, they now really serve just as an addition to the text, not the eye-catcher.<br><br>
Surfing forums about watches is not so funny anymore, I stopped it. I quit other social nets long time ago, watch forums was my last langolier (time eater).<br><br>
The most important - the phone became a tool. You take it out of pocket with particular task, you do this task and put it back. It does not distract. Not trying to entertain you. Or take your time. In short - <br><em>You use the phone when you need it, not when you feel bored. Not every minute.</em><br><br>
But screen time will be quite big, as I've started reading on phone. I have e-book, but it is comparatively big. Probably I will switch to another one later. Some smaller one, like 4 inch display. To save eyesight.
